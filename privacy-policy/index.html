Last updated: 8 September 2026

EarnifyX Lab ("we", "us", "our") operates the website earnifyxlab.in. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the website you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, mobile number and a password. Authentication and account storage are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Passwords are never stored by us in readable form. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.

Usage information

Saved items, bookmarks, recently viewed items, downloads and your light/dark theme preference are stored in your browser's local storage so the site can remember them between visits. This information stays on your device unless you are signed in, in which case account details are synced to Firebase.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites we automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and referring URL. We use Google Analytics (via Firebase Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

  • To improve the website, its content and its performance.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To protect the website against abuse, spam and fraud (for example using CAPTCHA and Firebase App Check).

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
